Privacy

Effective August 30, 2026

Rhythm Pedal Tidy processes MIDI performance data in your browser. Notes, pedal events, scores, and take names stay in IndexedDB on your device.

Files and MIDI devices

Imported files are read locally. Live recording listens only to the MIDI input you select. Your browser controls MIDI permission.

Exported files go to your browser’s download location.

Demo data

The sample demo uses a separate IndexedDB database. Reset demo replaces only its sample. Start for real clears the demo database.

Analytics and deletion

This version has no analytics, advertising, cookies, payment code, or tracking scripts.

Delete a take from the Take shelf. You can also clear all site data in your browser.

Contact

Questions can be filed in the public source repository.
