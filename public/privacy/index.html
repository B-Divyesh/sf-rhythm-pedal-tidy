Privacy

Effective August 27, 2026

Rhythm Pedal Tidy processes MIDI performance data in your browser. Notes, pedal events, scores, and take names are stored in IndexedDB on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Files and MIDI devices

Imported files are read locally. Live recording uses the browser’s Web MIDI permission and only listens to the input you select while recording. Exported files go wherever your browser saves downloads.

Licenses

If you buy Plus, checkout is hosted by Sociobot and its merchant of record, Dodo. This app stores your license token and a time-stamped verification result in local storage. It sends that token to the Sociobot verification endpoint no more than once per day. We do not receive payment-card details.

Analytics and deletion

This version has no analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts. Delete individual takes in the Take shelf, clear the app’s site data in your browser, or remove the license in the Unlock section.

Contact

Questions can be filed in the project’s public source repository linked from the app footer.
