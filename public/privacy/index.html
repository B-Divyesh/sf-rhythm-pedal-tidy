Privacy

Effective August 30, 2026

Rhythm Pedal Tidy processes MIDI performance data in your browser. Notes, pedal events, scores, and take names are stored in IndexedDB on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Files and MIDI devices

Imported files are read locally. Live recording uses the browser’s Web MIDI permission and only listens to the input you select while recording. Exported files go wherever your browser saves downloads.

Demo data

The sample demo uses a separate IndexedDB database. Resetting the demo or choosing Start for real clears that sample data. The demo does not read or write your real take shelf.

Analytics and deletion

This version has no analytics, advertising, cookies, payments, or tracking scripts. Delete individual takes in the Take shelf or clear the app’s site data in your browser.

Contact

Questions can be filed in the project’s public source repository linked from the app footer.
